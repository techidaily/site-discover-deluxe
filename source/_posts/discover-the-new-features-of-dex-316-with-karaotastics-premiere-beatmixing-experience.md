## Discover the New Features of Dex 3.16 with Karaotastic's Premiere Beatmixing Experience!

[![DEX 3.16 sneak peek](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/dex16sneakpeak-1.jpg?resize=775%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/dex16sneakpeak-1.jpg?fit=775%2C480&ssl=1 "DEX 3.16 sneak peek")

If you’re a member of the Official PCDJ Facebook User Group you already know (if you’re not, [join!](http://www.facebook.com/groups/pcdjusergroup/)), here’s a sneak peek at a couple of the new karaoke-specific features you will receive in the forthcoming 3.16 update.

We’re not going to delve too deeply into describing the new capabilities. As they say, a picture speaks a thousand lyrics…errr, words.

As the title of this post suggests, our goal in offering worlds-best karaoke software in [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [LYRX](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com/), is nearing one-step closer. You’ve been asking, we’ve been listening, and a myriad of new karaoke-specific features are on their way, just in time as many states open back up and karaoke hosts get back to doing what they do best.

Take a gander at the images below and expect an announcement in the coming weeks. As always, if you’re a DEX 3 or LYRX owner the updates will be free of charge. If you’re not an owner of either product — what are you waiting for?!? Learn more about [LYRX](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com/) or [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)
