We’re not going to delve too deeply into describing the new capabilities. As they say, a picture speaks a thousand lyrics…errr, words.

You’ve been asking, we’ve been listening, and a myriad of new karaoke-specific features are on their way, just in time as many states open back up and karaoke hosts get back to doing what they do best.

Take a gander at the images below and expect an announcement in the coming weeks. As always, if you’re a DEX 3 or LYRX owner the updates will be free of charge.
